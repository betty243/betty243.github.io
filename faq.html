Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Orders are dispatched within 1-2 business days and usually arrive within 4-7 business days across India.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is free on orders above the free-shipping amount shown in your cart. Below that, a small flat fee applies.

Can I return a product?

Yes. Unused items in original packaging can be returned within 7 days of delivery. See our Refund & Return Policy for details.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, tracking details are sent to your email or phone number.

What if I receive a damaged item?

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item and packaging, and we will arrange a replacement or refund.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We are currently setting up our online payment system. Available payment options will be shown at checkout once it is live.

How can I contact support?

Email us or call during business hours. Our details are on the Contact page.
